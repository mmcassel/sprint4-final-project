# Sprint 4 Final Project: E-Commerce Customer Behavior EDA

Exploratory data analysis of the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset (about 99,000 orders, 2016–2018), using Python, pandas, SQL (SQLite) and matplotlib.

## Key findings
- 97% of orders were successfully delivered; order volume grew steadily through 2017 and peaked in November 2017.
- Health & beauty is the top revenue category and also has one of the highest review scores.
- Customers are heavily concentrated in São Paulo and southeastern Brazil.
- Faster delivery is linked to better reviews: 5-star orders arrived in about 10 days on average, compared with about 21 days for 1-star orders.
